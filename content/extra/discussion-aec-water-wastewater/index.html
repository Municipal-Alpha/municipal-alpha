The method, briefly

Every city, town, and district publishes these decisions before they happen, scattered across hundreds of separate town websites, almost none of which reach a search engine. I read them the night they post and verify every signal against the source document. It's not an AI-first tool, it's a deterministic navigator I built that knows where each government's records live. The model labels a document once it's in hand, it isn't the moat.

Two questions worth answering before the call

Build vs. buy
"We already watch our market. Our people hear about this work."

They do, and this doesn't replace them. It replaces the reading you'd otherwise have to do across the hundreds of towns you aren't already watching, where you don't have a relationship yet.

What it won't do
"So this wins us the work?"

No, and any tool that says it can is overselling. It surfaces the sourced signal early, with real lead time. Whether that becomes a win is on your people, your past performance, and your price.
