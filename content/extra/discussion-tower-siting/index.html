Why FAA and FCC feeds miss this

FAA antenna-structure registrations and FCC license filings are downstream, by the time an entry is filed, the lease is executed and the site selected. Wireless Estimator, BeAccurate, and adjacent FCC-derived feeds work that layer well. What they can't answer is when and where the next application is forming, who's filing, and how the town handles these applications, the layer this reads.
