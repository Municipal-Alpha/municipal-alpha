The problem

Bid channels show you the field. You want the room before the field exists.

The RFP is the end of a long public process: a board names a problem, a committee forms, engineering opinions get requested, a capital line appears. Every step is in minutes and packets — town by town, unread.

In many towns the RFP never comes at all. The work is awarded out of the process above, to whoever was already in the conversation.
