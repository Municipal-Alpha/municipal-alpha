THE HUMANS BEHIND THE PDFS

Meet the operators

Two people and a very large pile of PDFs. The product holds records from 4,800+ municipalities in 51 states and keeps 2,300+ towns on its reading rounds, so we don't have to. We spend our time on the fun part: learning how your business works and making sure what reaches you is worth your time.

FOUNDER OF THE MONTH

Matt MacDonald

BUILT THE THING · PORTLAND, MAINE

Matt has spent nearly three decades taming messy, scattered information into data products: first on the early web, then in audio, now in town records. He first tried to make town documents readable with NearbyFYI more than a decade ago. The documents won that round. This time he's winning.

  • Chief Product Officer at Acast, through its NASDAQ listing
  • Co-founder of RadioPublic, later acquired by Acast
  • Chief Product Officer at PRX
  • Embedded at the Roux Institute, Northeastern University

Christian Milz
