Mississippi

Mississippi has no gatekeeper. The Governor's office judged every application on its own and paid providers directly, so the buyers are the named awardees, and 27 of them won money specifically for telehealth hubs and equipment.

Mississippi Rural Health Transformation Program · Direct competitive subgrants to individual providers, reimbursed after spending. · Next dated decision: Workforce and psychiatric emergency awards within 30 to 45 days of 14 September 2026; more opportunities in October 2026. Federal: 2027 amount by 31 October 2026.

Missouri

Missouri is the consortium shape Luke described: 27 hubs, each with an anchor that decides locally. Telehealth is the one piece still in market research, so the state is choosing how to buy it right now.

ToRCH Care (Transformation of Rural Community Health Care) · Regional hubs. 27 Community Hub Areas, each with one Hub Anchor organization, under regional coordinating networks. · Next dated decision: Federal: CMS sets Missouri's 2027 amount by 31 October 2026. The state's telehealth request for information closed 9 September 2026 and is for planning only.

Maine

Maine has no consortium to sell into. The state holds the money and routes telehealth through one lead contractor, which subcontracts named vendors. The opening that fits a portal is the Telehealth Hubs line: rooms in community buildings, and several Maine libraries are already building them.

Rural Health Transformation Program · Direct from the state. DHHS pays eligible providers itself and has contracted MCD Global Health to run telehealth and AI work, with named subcontractors. · Next dated decision: Federal: CMS sets Maine's 2027 amount by 31 October 2026. No Maine document states a separate 2027 round.

Kiosks and portals are not named anywhere in the federal notice or FAQ. They fit its categories for consumer-facing technology and IT hardware, which is a reading of those categories, not a quotation.
