How to read this page

These rankings measure what our coverage saw spread, not what spread. 36 of the 191 Maine municipalities in our corpus produced a tracked topic event on a document dated inside the window; growing the corpus grows the numbers. If a subject's rank rises in a window where its new-adopter count rose no faster than the covered-town population, that movement is a coverage artifact, not adoption.

Dates are each document's own meeting date, never the date we found it. Organization rankings count deliberative records only; sightings without a document date are excluded and counted below.
