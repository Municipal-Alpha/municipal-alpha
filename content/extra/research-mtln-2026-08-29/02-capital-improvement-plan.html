OUR READ

What this would mean for someone living here. Our words, not the town's.

If you have children who use town recreation programs, or you use town recreation facilities, the town will prioritize youth participation and retention, and maintaining and improving facilities, with new bond funding. Serves: Update me, Help me.

UPDATE ME, A STARTING DRAFT

A paragraph a reporter can lightly edit and run.

Capital Improvement Plan. The Waterville Parks and Recreation Advisory Committee is developing a Strategic Plan to guide the use of approximately $750,000 in Recreation Development Bond funding. The committee agreed that youth participation and retention should be the top priority, while also recognizing the importance of maintaining and improving recreational facilities. "Director Skehan explained that approximately $750,000 remains in Recreation Development Bond funding and that the committee's goal is to identify the best use of those funds." (Waterville minutes, 16 July).

WHAT IT IMPLICATES

What this matter changes elsewhere in the town, from the taxonomy's chains.

The Capital Improvement Plan, specifically the Amazon distribution center project, will increase the number of public fire hydrants along Trafton Road, which will increase the City's water main extension costs. This will contribute to the debt service growth noted in the FY2027 Draft Budget, which is a primary cost driver for the municipal budget.

  • The City of Waterville must approve of the number and location of public fire hydrants added along Trafton Road as part of the necessary water main extension to supply the proposed development (E1).
  • The Developer must execute a Water Main Extension Agreement with KWD and provide KWD with Trafton Road water main extension design plans for review and approval that meet the requirements of KWD’s Water Main Extension Agreement (E1).
  • Debt service growth as prior bond issuances shift from interest-only payments to combined principal and interest is a primary cost driver for the FY2027 municipal budget (R2).
  • three or four new public fire hydrants (E1)
  • Debt service growth (R2)
