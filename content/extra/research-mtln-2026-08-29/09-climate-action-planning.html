Harpswell · 29 July, Board of Selectmen

The Select Board was scheduled to hold a workshop and a presentation on a future flood risk study.

Our readnewsletter item. The future flood risk study involves the entire Town of Harpswell.

Union · 4 August, Unknown

The Select Board was scheduled to consider approving a Request for Proposals for a multimodal transportation improvements plan for the Union Village Common area, which seeks to identify infrastructure improvements and policy recommendations to enhance pedestrian safety, bicycle connectivity, accessibility, traffic calming, and overall multimodal mobility, as well as the long-term climate resilience and sustainability of the mobility network. The project, funded through a Maine Community Resilience Partnership Community Action Grant, has a budget of up to $70,000 for consultant services, with proposals due by September 17, 2026.

Our readlead-story candidate. The project has a budget of up to $70,000 and aims to improve the Union Village Common area, affecting many residents.

Blue Hill · 6 August

The Blue Hill Observatory has analyzed historical trends in temperature, moisture, sunshine, and wind speed since 1885, showing a strong increase in temperature, increasing water vapor, a decline in wind speed since 1980, and decadal changes in sunshine duration.

Our readnewsletter item. The analysis covers data from the Blue Hill Observatory since 1885.

Falmouth · 10 August, Town Council

The Town Council is considering amendments to the Code of Ordinances regarding poultry, setback variances for single-family dwellings, and municipal green building requirements for new construction, major renovations, and energy retrofits to comply with state mandates and the Falmouth Climate Action Plan.

Our readlead-story candidate. The ordinance amendments affect all districts regarding poultry, single-family dwellings for setback variances, and all Town of Falmouth owned or occupied buildings for green building requirements.

South Portland · 17 August, Conservation Commission - Regular Meeting (HYBRID)
