UPDATE ME, A STARTING DRAFT

A paragraph a reporter can lightly edit and run.

Lewiston Council weighs extending mobile home park rent moratorium. The Lewiston City Council was scheduled to consider extending a moratorium ordinance regarding mobile home lot rent increases for an additional 179 days. The Housing Committee has been working to prepare a draft ordinance to regulate mobile home lot rent increases and requested additional time to complete their work. A mobile home park owner spoke against a rent control ordinance at a July 21 meeting, stating it is not business-friendly and will prevent park expansion. "That the proposed Moratorium Ordinance regarding Rent Increases for Lots in Mobile Home Parks, to be effective September 29, 2026, receive first passage by a roll call vote" (City Council - agenda packet, 25 August).
