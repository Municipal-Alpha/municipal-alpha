What a wider run would cover

Five hundred districts run on the same system as fourteen

Nothing here gets harder as the number goes up. BoardDocs works the same way in every district that runs it, so a wider run is the same reading, just more of it. What the number changes is the price and how quickly it lands.

Six of these fourteen are showing a pre-RFP signal right now, a feasibility poll, a master plan update, a resolution seeking an advisor. At fourteen districts that is six live openings, and the number you pick is what that figure gets multiplied by.

Credit-worthiness and cabinet-level personnel, your use cases four and five, are sitting in these same records and I have not built them yet, so they are part of what a wider run would buy rather than something missing from it.

This is a sample, not a ceiling

What is not in here, I left out on purpose

The fourteen districts, the window, the two of your six use cases I built, those are choices I made for a few days of work rather than the edge of what the system reaches. OCR runs nightly across the rest of the corpus, I just did not point it at these fourteen districts, so fourteen of these registers are still scans rather than text. I read agendas rather than minutes, so most rows show what staff recommended and not the recorded vote, and that is a fetch away rather than a gap. Where a firm looks light on payments in a district, that is usually a page I have not turned rather than a firm that is not there.

The one thing that is not a choice is how a board agenda reads. An item saying "Award of RFP to Vendor X" is what staff put in front of the board, not what the board did. Paid warrants are the exception, a check has actually gone out.
