The One North lagoon site

Twenty-seven acres of former paper-mill treatment lagoons inside a 1,379-acre industrial park. The park has its own lake intake and a river outfall already permitted for a large land-based fish farm. A federal rail grant covers rebuilding track into the park.

Our Katahdin, which owns the park, leases land in it to industrial companies through One North. If your operation needs a lot of water in and out, rail and industrial zoning, the lagoon site is worth a call: info@onenorth.net or 207-249-8578.

Who the site suits

The discharge permit was written for a 10,000-tonne-a-year land-based salmon farm, allowing 4.0 million gallons a day through the old mill's diffuser outfall into a Class B stream. The state found that flow to be about 0.3 percent of the stream's regulated minimum. That permit, the intake design and the outfall suit another land-based aquaculture operation first, then water-intensive processing. (Our reading of the permit, not the state's.)

The permit is held by the salmon company and does not move with the land. Under its standard conditions it transfers only with the Department of Environmental Protection's approval.
