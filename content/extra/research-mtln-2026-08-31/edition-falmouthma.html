Why this looks different

This edition is built on what three of you said about the last ones, not timely, not newsworthy, mostly already known, and on what Scott asked for on Friday: lean harder into the read, point at what else the record could support, and line the brief up with the six reader needs the newsroom sorts stories by.

What changed, in the order you will see it. A thread you have seen before says so in its first line, and only the documents added since the last edition are marked NEW. An item that is only Update Me, the kind you were in the room for, is listed in one line at the end rather than written up again. Under each thread's read there is a short block called Angles the record supports. It names the other needs the record could serve, Educate Me, Give Me Perspective, Help Me, and points to the document each one rests on. Each thread also says whether a meeting or hearing is ahead or whether it runs any week.

What the record says is still only the record, quoted where it can be. The read and the angles are ours, and say so.

Two asks. If an angle is wrong, or is not what Scott meant by other perspectives, reply and say so, and the next edition changes. If there are topics you want weighted up or down, send Scott the list and the brief will name the source when it applies.

Falmouth considers ordinance amendments for state mandates

NEW: 1 document3 meetings, 6 weeksno date set, runs any week

If you manage or work in a Town-owned or occupied building, the town will prohibit new or replacement fossil fuel systems for regulated loads in energy retrofit projects starting July 13. Serves: Help me, Update me.

Continuing from the 24 August edition, 1 new document since.

What the record says, angles, 3 documents

What the record says

Falmouth will introduce amendments to Chapters II-19 and II-24 of the Code of Ordinances on July 13 to comply with state mandates regarding poultry, setback variances, and energy systems. The Community Development Committee (CDC) recommended these amendments for adoption after reviewing them at its May 12, 2026 meeting. The Town Council held its public hearing on August 10, 2026, and the Planning Board held its MRA hearing on August 4, 2026. The ordinance prohibits new or replacement fossil fuel systems for regulated loads in Town-owned or occupied buildings as part of energy retrofit projects.

Give me perspective: The Town's Zoning Map is an attachment, providing a visual context for how the proposed amendments might affect different areas. (13 July packet)

Help me: The proposed ordinance amendment language is available, allowing residents to review the exact changes to the Code of Ordinances. (13 July packet)
