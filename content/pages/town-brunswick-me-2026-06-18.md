## Town Government

The Brunswick Town Council met on Tuesday, June 14, to address several key municipal initiatives, most notably moving forward with the disposition of the former Hawthorne School property. The council voted unanimously to issue a Request for Proposals for the redevelopment of the vacant school building, signaling a significant step toward its future use. Town Manager John Smith indicated that proposals, which are anticipated to include options for affordable housing and mixed-use development, will be due by August 15, 2026. The Hawthorne School Redevelopment Task Force will then review submissions and provide recommendations to the council. (June 14, 2026 Town Council Agenda Packet)

Earlier in the week, on Monday, June 13, the Brunswick Staff Review Committee convened to evaluate ongoing development and infrastructure projects. The committee gave preliminary approval for a shoreland stabilization project along the Maquoit Bay coastline, near the end of Mere Point Road. This project aims to mitigate erosion and protect sensitive coastal habitats. Additionally, the committee reviewed engineering plans for a new traffic signal installation at the intersection of Old Bath Road and Church Road, a measure intended to improve traffic flow and pedestrian safety in the growing area. (June 13, 2026 Staff Review Committee Meeting Minutes)

The Planning Board held its regular meeting on Wednesday, June 15, where it considered two significant development proposals. The board reviewed an application for the Thomas Point Campground III expansion, which proposes adding 20 new recreational vehicle sites and associated amenities off Thomas Point Road. Following a public hearing, the board requested additional information regarding stormwater management plans. The board also conditionally approved the Wilbur's Woods II residential development, a plan for 15 new single-family homes on a 10-acre parcel off Wilbur Way, pending finalization of lot line adjustments and open space designations. (June 15, 2026 Planning Board Agenda Packet)

## Schools

The Brunswick School Board met on Wednesday, June 15, at the Harriet Beecher Stowe Elementary School to discuss summer programming and upcoming facility needs. Superintendent Jane Doe presented an update on projected enrollment for the 2026-2027 academic year, showing a slight increase at the elementary level. The board approved funding for several summer enrichment programs, including a robotics camp at Brunswick Junior High School and a literacy initiative across the elementary schools, totaling $45,000. Discussions also included the initial planning stages for a capital improvement project to upgrade the athletic fields at Brunswick High School, with a proposed budget of $250,000 for initial design work in the next fiscal year.

## Police

The Brunswick Police Department responded to 187 calls for service between June 12 and June 18. Incidents included a motor vehicle accident on Route 1 near the Cooks Corner intersection on Friday, June 12, resulting in minor injuries and traffic delays. Officers also investigated a report of theft from a retail establishment on Maine Street on Sunday, June 14, where several electronics items were reported stolen. Patrols addressed a noise complaint originating from a residence near the Bowdoin College campus on Monday, June 15, with residents advised of the town's noise ordinance.

## Fire & EMS

The Brunswick Fire Department and Emergency Medical Services handled 62 calls during the past week. Fire personnel responded to a medical emergency at a business within Brunswick Landing on Saturday, June 13, transporting one individual to Mid Coast Hospital. On Tuesday, June 16, crews extinguished a small brush fire in a wooded area off Harpswell Road, quickly containing it before it could spread. The department also provided mutual aid to the Freeport Fire Department for a structure fire on Main Street in Freeport on Thursday, June 18.
