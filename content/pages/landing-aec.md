<h3>The signal chain</h3>
<p>Municipal capital work follows a predictable document trail. A facilities assessment or master plan names the problem. A budget workshop attaches a number to it. A capital improvement plan schedules it. A warrant article or bond vote funds it. Then, often a year or more after the first document, an RFP formalizes what everyone in the room already knew. Each step is public, published to a different board's portal. I connect them, so you see the water system upgrade, the school renovation, or the remediation scope while the project is still being shaped and the town is still open to conversation.</p>
</div>
