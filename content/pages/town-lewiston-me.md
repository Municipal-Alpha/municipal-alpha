*Lewiston, Maine. Androscoggin County.*

## What this page is

This is a record of what Municipal Alpha holds from Lewiston's own published documents. It is built from the corpus itself, so every count below is a count of documents we actually have.

It is deliberately not a narrative. Where a narrative appears on a page like this one, every sentence in it is read from the full text of a named document and the document is cited. Until that reading has been done for this city, this page states the shape of the record rather than describing it.

**Last built:** 2026-08-30.

## What we hold

- **5,968 documents** in total.
- **179** carry a meeting date in 2026.
- **1,371** (23 percent) had their text extracted successfully and are readable to us. The rest are held as a title and a link only. Nothing can be said about the contents of those.
- **84 boards and committees** appear in the record.
- **3,492** have aged out of local storage. We know they exist and where they were; we no longer hold their text.
- **388** are scanned images awaiting text recognition.
- The most recent document was discovered on **2026-08-28**. Anything the city published after that date is outside this page.
- **736** documents carry no usable meeting date, so they do not appear in any date-ordered view below.

## Corrections

Two kinds of correction are useful. A fact that is wrong, and a thread that matters to Lewiston and is missing here because we cannot see the document it lives in. Both make the next version better. Where this page says the record stops, that is a statement about what we hold, not about the city.
