Summary: In three towns, the first PFAS record sat in the town’s own minutes 13 to 33 months before settlement money arrived or a treatment plant started running. The next ones are on agendas now.

<h2 style="font-family:'Source Serif 4',serif; font-size:24px; font-weight:500; margin:0 0 14px;">The pattern</h2>
<p style="font-size:15px; color:oklch(0.75 0.01 60); margin:0 0 32px;">PFAS, the “forever chemicals,” reach a town’s agenda first as a lawsuit, a spill or a test result. The money follows: settlement checks, treatment plants, new wells, higher water rates. Both ends are decided in the same council chambers, and both are written down.</p>
<h2 style="font-family:'Source Serif 4',serif; font-size:24px; font-weight:500; margin:0 0 14px;">From the first record to the money</h2>
<p style="margin:0 0 28px; font-family:'Source Serif 4',serif; font-size:clamp(26px,4.5vw,34px); line-height:1.25; color:oklch(0.93 0.01 60);"><span style="color:oklch(0.78 0.14 75);">13 to 33 months</span> from the first PFAS record in a town’s minutes to money in hand or a treatment plant running.</p>

<h2 style="font-family:'Source Serif 4',serif; font-size:24px; font-weight:500; margin:0 0 14px;">The early signal: a year at the public-comment microphone</h2>
<p style="font-size:15px; color:oklch(0.75 0.01 60); margin:0 0 16px;">Manchester, New Hampshire shows what the first record looks like. Residents kept coming back to the Board of Mayor and Aldermen to ask the city to keep PFAS out of its wastewater plant. A year later the ordinance was on the table, sponsored in part by one of the residents who had spoken.</p>

<h2 style="font-family:'Source Serif 4',serif; font-size:24px; font-weight:500; margin:0 0 14px;">Why it matters</h2>
<p style="font-size:15px; color:oklch(0.75 0.01 60); margin:0 0 32px;">Every PFAS decision carries a budget line: testing, engineering, treatment, legal work and the borrowing that pays for it. If you sell to water utilities or help towns pay for infrastructure, the next one is already in a town’s minutes. Tell us your market in the box below, and we will look for it there.</p>
